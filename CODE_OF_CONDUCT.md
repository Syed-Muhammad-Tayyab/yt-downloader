# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level or nationality.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions kindly.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with care.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Report concerns through the maintainer's GitHub profile: <https://github.com/Syed-Muhammad-Tayyab>.
